We've rebuilt the College of Engineering HPC site from the ground up. Highlights:

- A **live cluster status** dashboard on the homepage, refreshed automatically
  from Slurm (`sinfo`/`squeue`) every few minutes.
- Updated [Getting Started](/getting-started/) and [Documentation](/documentation/)
  guides, including example batch scripts for CPU, GPU, and big-memory jobs.
- This News section, for maintenance windows, new hardware, and software
  updates.
